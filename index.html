How to play

A memory game. Watch the pads light up in order, then repeat the sequence. It grows by one step every round.

  1. Press Start game.
  2. Watch and listen as the four pads flash in sequence.
  3. When it is your turn, click the pads in the same order.
  4. Each round you complete adds one more step to the pattern.
  5. One wrong pad ends the run. Your best round is saved.
